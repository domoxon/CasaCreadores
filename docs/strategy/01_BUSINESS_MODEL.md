# 01 — Business Model
**Casa Creadores**
_LATAM UGC Creator Network for Tech & Crypto Brands_

---

## What We Are

Casa Creadores is a managed creator network connecting LATAM-based content creators with tech and crypto brands that want authentic, local-language UGC at scale. We are not a traditional influencer agency — we focus on user-generated content (UGC), engagement quality, and conversion outcomes, not just impressions.

We operate as three things simultaneously:
1. A **sales agency** — outbound to brands, pitch decks, campaign proposals
2. A **talent network** — recruiting, vetting, and managing LATAM creators
3. A **tech platform** — website, calculator tool, campaign reporting, Discord community

---

## Core Differentiators

- **LATAM-only focus** — no US/EU agency understands or serves this market well
- **LinkedIn as a primary channel** — unique in the creator marketing space; targets B2B crypto/tech buyers
- **Crypto/tech vertical expertise** — not a generalist agency, deep niche credibility
- **Stablecoin-native payments** — creators paid in USDT/USDC, solving LATAM banking friction
- **Bilingual operation** — Spanish and Portuguese, not translated English

---

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Campaign Fees (Primary)
Brand pays a flat campaign fee. Casa Creadores takes a 25–35% margin and passes the remainder to creators.

Example:
- Brand pays $1,500 for a campaign
- Casa Creadores margin: $450 (30%)
- Creator pool payout: $1,050 split across creators

### 2. Platform/Management Fee (Secondary)
For ongoing brand relationships, charge a monthly retainer for creator access and campaign management on top of per-post creator costs.

- Entry retainer: $300–$500/mo
- Growth retainer: $800–$1,500/mo

### 3. Discord Community (Tertiary)
Paid creator membership for first access to brand briefs, LATAM market intelligence, and peer network.

- Creator membership: $10–$25/mo
- More detail in `07_DISCORD_COMMUNITY.md`

## Business Stages

### Stage 0 — Pre-Launch (Now)
- Build brand identity (logo, X account, website)
- Recruit founding creator cohort (20–30 creators, hand-picked)
- Build and launch website with calculator
- Set up Notion ops workspace

### Stage 1 — Validation (Months 1–3)
- Run 3 free/low-cost pilot campaigns
- Collect case study data and testimonials
- Refine creator vetting and campaign process
- Begin paid outreach to brands

### Stage 2 — Revenue (Months 4–8)
- 3–5 paid campaigns/month
- Launch Discord community
- Introduce retainer packages

### Stage 3 — Scale (Month 9+)
- Build client-facing campaign dashboard
- Expand creator roster to 100+
- Introduce performance-based pricing option
- Consider LinkedIn-specific campaign packages

## Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| No creators at launch | Hand-recruit 20–30 founding creators before opening applications |
| No brands at launch | 3 free pilots to build case studies before charging |
| Creator payment friction in LATAM | USDT/USDC via Binance Pay as default payout method |
| Crypto content compliance | Clear brand ToS, liability on brand not Casa Creadores |
| API scraper reliability | Cache results, have fallback providers on RapidAPI |
| LinkedIn monetisation uncertainty | Educate brands on LinkedIn B2B creator value, price as premium |
