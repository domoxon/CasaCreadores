## Creator Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1 — Founding Cohort (Pre-Launch)
Do NOT open public applications yet. Hand-recruit 20–30 creators personally through:
- Business partner's Instagram/TikTok/X network (primary channel)
- Direct DMs to creators who already post about crypto/fintech in Spanish/Portuguese
- LinkedIn outreach to LATAM professionals with engaged followings
- Referrals from first recruits ("do you know anyone else who...")

**Goal:** Have 20–30 vetted, briefed, and excited founding creators before the website launches. This solves the chicken-and-egg problem — brands see a roster from day one.

### Phase 2 — Open Applications (Post-Launch)
Once the website is live, the calculator tool itself becomes a creator acquisition funnel. A creator uses the calculator, sees their estimated earnings, and is prompted to apply to the network.

CTA on calculator results page:
> "You could be earning $X per post. Apply to join the Casa Creadores network."

### Phase 3 — Referral Programme
Existing creators refer new creators. Small cash bonus or priority deal access for successful referrals.

## Vetting Criteria

Every creator application is reviewed against these criteria before acceptance:

### Mandatory
- [ ] Public profile on at least one listed platform
- [ ] Content is in Spanish or Portuguese (or bilingual)
- [ ] At least 50% of content is relevant to tech, crypto, finance, or entrepreneurship
- [ ] Engagement rate meets minimum threshold for their tier (see below)
- [ ] No history of promoting scam/rug pull projects
- [ ] Willing to accept USDT/USDC payment or has a bank account we can reach

### Engagement Rate Minimums
| Tier | Platform | Minimum ER |
|---|---|---|
| Nano (1K–10K) | Instagram / TikTok | 3% |
| Micro (10K–100K) | Instagram / TikTok | 1.5% |
| Mid (100K+) | Instagram / TikTok | 0.8% |
| Any tier | LinkedIn | 1% (lower due to platform norms) |
| Any tier | YouTube | 2% (likes+comments/views) |

### Red Flags — Auto Reject
- Follower-to-engagement ratio that suggests purchased followers
- Sudden follower spikes in audit history (HypeAuditor free check)
- Previously promoted known scam tokens without disclosure
- Private accounts with no verifiable metrics
- Content entirely unrelated to our verticals

## Creator Application Flow

1. Creator finds Casa Creadores (organic, referral, or calculator CTA)
2. Fills in application form (see fields below)
3. Casa Creadores reviews within 5 business days
4. If approved: creator receives welcome email + onboarding doc
5. Creator added to internal Notion roster
6. Creator invited to Discord community (paid tier or free founding tier TBD)

### Application Form Fields
- Full name
- Country
- Primary platform + handle
- All other platform handles
- Follower counts (self-reported, we verify)
- Content niche (dropdown: crypto, DeFi, fintech, tech, entrepreneurship, LinkedIn professional)
- Language(s) of content
- Payment preference (USDT wallet address, bank transfer, other)
- Short bio (used on website if featured)
- Link to 3 best performing posts
- Have you previously worked with brands? (Y/N + details)
- Agree to terms of service (checkbox)

## Creator Contracts — Key Clauses to Include

When a creator is assigned to a campaign, they sign (or digitally agree to) a brief that includes:

- Deliverable: exact number of posts, platforms, format
- Timeline: draft submission date, revision window, go-live date
- Content guidelines: brand tone, mandatory disclosures (#ad, #publicidad), prohibited claims
- Crypto compliance note: no price predictions, no "guaranteed returns" language
- Usage rights: brand may repurpose content for paid ads (must be specified upfront)
- Payment terms: paid within X days of post going live + screenshot submitted
- Exclusivity window: no competitor brand posts for X days before/after

## Creator Payment Model

**Default payment method:** USDT (TRC-20 or ERC-20) or USDC
**Alternatives:** Binance Pay, bank transfer (country-dependent), Wise

**Payment schedule:**
- 50% on content approval (before posting) — builds trust with creators
- 50% on post confirmation + metrics screenshot submitted

**Why 50/50:** Creators in LATAM have been burned by brands that ghost after posting. Paying half upfront differentiates you and builds loyalty fast.

## Creator Retention Strategy

- Pay reliably and on time (this alone puts you in the top 10% of agencies)
- Give feedback on content performance after every campaign
- Celebrate wins publicly in Discord
- Offer repeat deal priority to best performers
- Monthly "LATAM Creator Digest" — market intel, brand trends, tips (delivered via Discord or email)
