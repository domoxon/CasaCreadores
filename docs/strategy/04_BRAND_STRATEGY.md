## How We Acquire Brand Clients

### Primary Channel — Direct Outreach (You)
- Build a list of crypto/tech brands actively expanding into LATAM
- Send personalised pitch deck via LinkedIn DM or email
- Target: founders, CMOs, Head of Growth, Marketing Managers
- Volume target: 20 outreach messages/week minimum

**Where to find leads:**
- CoinMarketCap / CoinGecko — exchanges and projects with LATAM pages or Spanish-language social accounts
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator search: "Head of Marketing" + "crypto" + "LATAM" or "Latin America"
- Twitter/X crypto communities in Spanish — brands already trying to reach LATAM but doing it badly
- AngelList / Crunchbase — funded LATAM fintech startups
- Conferences: LaBitConf, Cripto Latin Fest, Bitcoin Miami LATAM sessions

### Secondary Channel — Inbound (Website + Calculator)
- The calculator tool attracts brands researching LATAM creator costs
- Blog/SEO content about LATAM creator marketing (longer term)
- LinkedIn posts from your personal profile positioning you as the LATAM UGC expert

### Tertiary — Referrals
- Happy clients refer other brands
- Offer 10% credit on next campaign for successful referrals

## What to Include in a Pitch Deck

Keep it to 8–10 slides:

1. **The Problem** — crypto/tech brands can't reach LATAM audiences authentically
2. **The Market** — LATAM crypto adoption stats, market size, growth trajectory
3. **Our Solution** — Casa Creadores, what we do in one sentence
4. **Our Network** — creator roster size, platforms, countries, tier breakdown (use real numbers from founding cohort)
5. **LinkedIn Differentiation** — why LinkedIn UGC in LATAM is untapped and valuable for B2B
6. **How It Works** — 3-step process: brief → creators → results
7. **Campaign Packages** — Spark / Fuego / Escala pricing
8. **Case Studies / Social Proof** — even if early, use pilot campaign data or mock examples clearly labelled as projections
9. **The Team** — you and your partner, relevant experience
10. **Next Step** — book a call or email CTA

## Client Contract — Key Points

- Campaign brief is the binding document (deliverables, platforms, creator count, timeline)
- Payment: 50% deposit upfront, 50% on campaign completion
- Content usage rights: brand gets license to repurpose content for 12 months in paid channels
- Revision policy: one round of content feedback before posting
- Compliance: brand is responsible for ensuring campaign claims comply with local financial regulations
- Cancellation: deposit non-refundable if cancelled within 7 days of campaign start
- Reporting: delivered within 5 business days of campaign end

## Brand Onboarding Checklist

Once a brand signs and pays deposit:

- [ ] Send campaign brief template for them to fill in
- [ ] Confirm UTM link structure with their team (or create it for them)
- [ ] Match creators from roster to brief criteria
- [ ] Send creator shortlist to brand for approval (optional but builds trust)
- [ ] Creator contracts/briefs sent and confirmed
- [ ] Set up Metricool tracking for campaign
- [ ] Brief creators with all brand guidelines, key messages, prohibited language
- [ ] Confirm content submission and go-live dates

## Pricing Philosophy

**Don't compete on price.** The LATAM creator market is not price-sensitive at the brand level — brands are willing to pay for expertise and reliability. Competing on price attracts low-quality clients and undervalues the network.

**Lead with outcomes, not deliverables.** Don't sell "5 posts." Sell "5 trusted LATAM crypto voices driving sign-ups to your platform." The framing changes the perceived value entirely.

**LinkedIn content commands a premium.** Position LinkedIn UGC at 1.5–2x the rate of Instagram posts of the same follower count, due to the B2B decision-maker audience quality.
