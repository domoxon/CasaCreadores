# 07 — Discord Community
**Casa Creadores**

---

## Purpose

The Casa Creadores Discord serves two functions:
1. **Creator community** — a home for vetted LATAM crypto/tech creators to connect, collaborate, and access paid brand deals
2. **Revenue stream** — paid membership creates recurring income independent of campaign fees

The core value proposition for creators joining:
> "First access to paid brand deals, LATAM market intel, and a community of creators who speak your language and understand your market."

---

## Why Discord (Not WhatsApp, Telegram, or Slack)

- WhatsApp: no channel structure, not scalable, chaotic at 50+ members
- Telegram: fine but lacks community features, associated with crypto scams in creator minds
- Slack: business-feel, not creator-native, expensive at scale
- Discord: creator-native, channel structure, role-based access, bots for automation, free to run, familiar to crypto/tech communities

## Membership Tiers

### Free — Verified Creator
- Must have completed the creator application and been approved
- Access to: welcome, general creator channels
- No access to: deal flow, market intel, collab channels
- Purpose: get creators into the ecosystem, build community, upsell to paid

### Paid — Casa Insider
- Monthly fee: **$15/mo** (launch price, can increase later)
- Access to: all channels including deal flow and market intel
- Additional perks:
  - Monthly LATAM Creator Brief (market intel digest)
  - Priority consideration for brand campaigns
  - Direct feedback from Casa Creadores team on content
- Payment: via Stripe or crypto (USDT)

### Founding Member (First 50 paid members)
- Locked-in rate: **$10/mo for life** (or first 12 months)
- Badge + recognition in Discord
- One free campaign application review per quarter
- Purpose: incentivise early adoption and create urgency

## Bot Setup (When Ready)

Discord bots automate community management. Recommended bots:

| Bot | Purpose | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **MEE6** | Auto-roles, welcome messages, moderation | Free tier sufficient |
| **Carl-bot** | Reaction roles (let creators self-select platforms they're on) | Free |
| **Stripe/Whop integration** | Automated paid tier access on payment | Whop.com (~$0 + % fee) |

**Whop.com** is the recommended tool for gating paid Discord access. It handles:
- Payment collection (Stripe, crypto)
- Automatic role assignment on payment
- Cancellation and access removal
- Member management dashboard

Link: https://whop.com

## Launch Sequence for Discord

**Before website launches:**
1. Create Discord server with full channel structure
2. Set up MEE6 for welcome messages and rules enforcement
3. Set up Carl-bot for platform roles
4. Invite founding 20–30 creators as charter members (free)
5. Make it feel alive before opening it up — post in channels, have conversations

**At website launch:**
6. Add Discord link to website nav and creator pages
7. All new approved creators get free tier invite link
8. Announce paid Founding Member tier with urgency window (first 50 spots)
9. Set up Whop for paid tier gating

**30 days post-launch:**
10. Review what channels are active, archive dead ones
11. Post first monthly LATAM Creator Brief
12. Begin promoting Discord to creators on social

## Community Rules

To be posted in #rules-and-guidelines:

1. No self-promotion of personal services in general channels (use #collab-requests)
2. No discussion or promotion of unvetted/scam crypto projects
3. No sharing of brand briefs outside Discord
4. Treat all members with respect — zero tolerance for harassment
5. Paid deal information stays within the community
6. All content shared for feedback must be your own
