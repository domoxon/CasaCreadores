## Brand / Client Personas

### Persona 1 — Crypto Exchange or Wallet
- Launching in LATAM or expanding market share
- Needs local-language content that builds trust
- Cares about signups, app installs, KYC completions
- Budget: $500–$5,000/campaign
- Example brands: Bitget, OKX, Bybit, Lemon Cash, Bitso

### Persona 2 — DeFi / Web3 Protocol
- Needs technical credibility + community growth
- Wants creators who can explain complex products simply
- Cares about Discord/Telegram community growth, wallet connects
- Budget: $1,000–$10,000/campaign
- Example brands: Aave, Uniswap (LATAM push), local DeFi protocols

### Persona 3 — Fintech / Neobank
- Using crypto rails but marketing to mainstream users
- Needs relatable, non-technical content in Spanish/Portuguese
- Cares about signups, referral codes, brand awareness
- Budget: $500–$3,000/campaign
- Example brands: Nubank, Mercado Pago, Ualá, Prex

### Persona 4 — B2B Tech / SaaS (LinkedIn Focus)
- Targeting LATAM business decision-makers
- Needs LinkedIn creator content — thought leadership, product awareness
- Cares about demo requests, LinkedIn followers, brand authority
- Budget: $800–$5,000/campaign
- Example types: dev tools, cloud services, crypto infrastructure B2B

## Creator Personas

### Creator Tier 1 — Nano (1K–10K followers)
- Highest engagement rates (3–8%)
- Very niche, trusted community
- Will work for low fees ($20–$100/post) or product
- Great for early roster building

### Creator Tier 2 — Micro (10K–100K followers)
- Core of the network
- Balance of reach and trust
- Rate: $100–$500/post
- Most reliable for campaigns

### Creator Tier 3 — Mid (100K–500K followers)
- Aspirational for the network
- Harder to recruit without existing reputation
- Rate: $500–$3,000/post
- Priority target once Casa Creadores has brand recognition

### Creator Niches We Target
- Crypto / DeFi educators
- Personal finance in Spanish/Portuguese
- Tech reviewers and software creators
- LATAM startup / entrepreneur community
- LinkedIn professionals in fintech, banking, tech roles
- Female crypto creators (underserved, high engagement)
